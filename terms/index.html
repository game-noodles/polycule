3. Community guidelines

Polycule is a shared space for people in relationships with each other. Treat the people in it the way you’d want to be treated in your own home.

Welcome

  • Honest talk about relationships, feelings, sex, desire and intimacy between consenting adults. It’s part of relationships, and it’s allowed here.
  • Disagreement, done with care.

Not allowed

  • Anything sexual involving minors, in any form, including drawings, stories and jokes. We ban the account and report it to Cybertip.ca or the police.
  • Harassment or bullying, including sexual content aimed at someone who hasn’t welcomed it, and keeping on after someone has asked you to stop.
  • Threats or violence, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
  • Hate or discrimination against people for who they are, including their race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, relationship structure or disability.
  • Sharing private information without consent, including outing someone as poly, queer or trans, or sharing addresses, workplaces, intimate images or screenshots of private conversations.
  • Explicit drawings. Games are seen by everyone in the polycule, so keep drawings to things they’d all be comfortable seeing, and never draw a real person sexually without their consent.
  • Monitoring or controlling a partner, such as using the app to track, pressure or isolate someone.
  • Fake accounts, impersonation and spam, including advertising and selling things.
  • Anything illegal, and helping anyone else do something illegal.
  • False reports made to get someone into trouble.

People who aren’t in the app

You can add people to your polycule who don’t have an account. Only add someone who’d be comfortable being named, and remove them if they ask. Everyone in the polycule can see and edit them.

4. Reporting, blocking and bans

If someone breaks these guidelines, report them from their … menu on the People tab. Reports come to us, not to anyone in your polycule, and the person isn’t told who reported them. You can also mute or block them from the same menu. Neither is shown to them.

We read every report. We aim to look at reports of threats or of someone under 18 within a day, and the rest within a few days. Depending on what we find, we may:

  • do nothing, if no rule was broken;
  • contact the person with a warning;
  • remove what they wrote;
  • suspend or ban their account;
  • report them to the police or Cybertip.ca, where someone may be in danger or a child may be at risk, or where the law requires it.

We keep reports, including the email addresses of both accounts, even after an account is deleted. That’s so a banned person can be recognised if they come back.

A polycule’s admin can remove members from that polycule. That’s a decision for the people in it, not for us, and we don’t take part in it.

If we take action on your account and you think we’ve got it wrong, email us and we’ll look again.
