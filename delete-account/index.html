Delete your Polycule account

You can delete your Polycule account and its data at any time. Do this from inside the app, or by asking us here if you no longer have the app.

In the app (quickest)

  1. Open Polycule and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email us from the email address you use to sign in to Polycule, asking us to delete your account. We only act on requests from that address, so nobody else can delete your account for you.

Email a deletion request

Or write to support@wobblynoodles.com with the subject “Delete my Polycule account”.

We’ll delete the account within 30 days of your request, and reply to confirm when it’s done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, sign-in details, display name and avatar.
  • Your hearts, blossoms, store items and Premium record.
  • Your connections and connection requests, and your links to pets.
  • Nicknames you gave other people, and nicknames others gave you.
  • Your answers, game moves, activity-feed entries and invitations.
  • Your mutes and blocks, and other people’s mutes and blocks of you.
  • The devices registered to receive your notifications.

You leave every polycule you’re in. A polycule where you’re the only member is deleted with everything in it. Where you were the admin, the role passes to the next member.

What’s kept

  • Things you added that belong to a polycule. People added without an account, pets and finished games all stay for the other members, but no longer record that you added them.
  • Reports. Reports you made, and reports made about you, are kept, including the account’s id, name and email address at the time of the report. That’s so a banned person can be recognised if they come back. Reports are never shown to anyone in the app. See section 4 of our terms of use.
  • Backups. Copies of the database in our backups are overwritten within 30 days.
  • Payments. Google keeps its own records of Google Play purchases, under Google’s privacy policy.

If you subscribe to Premium

Premium is billed by Google Play, and deleting your account doesn’t cancel it. Cancel it first in the Play Store: Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Polycule → Cancel.
